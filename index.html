Good to Know

Common questions.

Do you supply materials or just labour?
Both. I can source gypsum boards, paint, wallpaper and panelling, or work with materials you've already bought.
How long does a gypsum ceiling take?
A standard room usually takes two to four days from framing to final finish, depending on size and design.
Do you work outside Nairobi?
Mostly Nairobi and the surrounding areas. Message me with your location and I'll confirm.
Is the estimate above the final price?
No, it's a rough guide. The final price is confirmed after a site visit and depends on finish and materials chosen.
